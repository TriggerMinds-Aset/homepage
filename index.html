Trigger Mind's Pop Culture × Coding event brought together students for a two-round quiz experience — first testing pop culture reflexes, then flipping those same references into beginner-friendly logic and code challenges.

Round One — Pop Culture Quiz

Fast-paced and all about quick recall across films, music, memes, games, and series.

A few things we've run before.

From domain-level coding contests to national-level events.

DOMAIN → NATIONAL LEVEL

DSA Dynamics 3.0

A hands-on platform to strengthen DSA skills through coding challenges on HackerRank, drawing 100+ expected participants from ASET, JP, DTU, AIIT, and IGDTU.

OUTCOME Participants sharpened problem-solving skills, deepened their understanding of data structures and algorithms, gained hands-on coding experience, and built stronger resumes.
DOMAIN LEVEL

Quizzitch

A multi-language coding quiz with questions ranging from basic to advanced, open to all years and played in teams of three — 60–70 registrations from ASET, AIIT, and AIDT.

OUTCOME Sharper understanding of OOP and Python concepts, with students solving problems spontaneously under quiz pressure.
INSTITUTE LEVEL

Resume Builder

A resume-building session focused on structure, content, and role-specific tailoring, with insights into what recruiters actually look for.

OUTCOME Stronger resumes, a clearer understanding of recruiter expectations, and better odds at internships and placements.
